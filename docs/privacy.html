Privacy Policy

Mojazer for Android · Effective 29 August 2026

Mojazer does not ask you for any personal information, and the developer does not collect any. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. Your worlds are saved on your own phone. The one exception is advertising, which is served by Google AdMob and is described in full below.

Who this is from

Mojazer is made and published by WatchMeDash, an independent developer. This policy covers the Android application com.watchmedash.mojazer distributed through Google Play.

What the game stores on your device

Mojazer keeps everything it needs locally, on the phone, and never sends any of it anywhere:

  • Saved worlds — terrain, your inventory, your character and your progress, in the save slots you choose.
  • Settings — controls, sound, graphics quality, weather and camera preferences.
  • Achievements and skills earned during play.

None of this leaves the device, and none of it is readable by the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Clearing the app's storage from Android Settings does the same without removing the app.

What the game does not do

  • No account, no login, no email address, no username.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • No precise location. The game never requests a location permission.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.
  • The game is fully playable offline; it needs a connection only to fetch an advert.

Advertising

Mojazer shows a single full-screen advert after your character dies — never during play, and not more than one every few minutes. Adverts are served by Google AdMob, which is a third party and collects data of its own to choose and measure them. Typically that includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and the fact that the advert was shown or tapped;
  • a coarse, city-level location derived from your IP address — not GPS, and not a permission the game asks for.

This data is handled by Google under its own policy, not by the developer, who never sees it. Google explains what it collects and why here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and its privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving a stable identifier for advertising.
  • Delete your data by uninstalling Mojazer, or by clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Mojazer → Storage. Because nothing is held on a server, there is no account for the developer to delete and no export to request.
  • Play offline — turn off mobile data and Wi-Fi and the game runs normally. No advert can load, and nothing is sent.

Children

Mojazer is not directed to children under 13 and is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It is not knowingly used to collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising described above, please get in touch and it will be addressed with the ad provider.

Security

Because no personal data is transmitted to or held by the developer, there is no server holding your information and no database that can be breached. Data on your phone is protected by Android's own app sandbox.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if a future version of the game adds a feature that needs data it does not need today — the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
