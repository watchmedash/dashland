MOJAZER — SOUND EFFECTS WANTED
==============================

What this is, and how the audio actually works, because it changes what to
look for.

Almost everything in this game is SYNTHESISED, not sampled. `src/audio/Audio.js`
is 3,300 lines of oscillators and noise bursts, and that is deliberate: a sound
played hundreds of times a session (footsteps, pickaxe swings, mob voices) needs
per-play randomisation, and a handful of recordings machine-gun. So a sample is
not a replacement for a synth voice here — it is a LAYER on top of it, or a
continuous bed underneath it.

There are already 17 samples doing exactly that (see part A). They set the bar:
short, dry, no reverb baked in, no music, and mono is fine. The engine pans and
attenuates them itself.

FORMAT
  Ogg Opus (.ogg) is what ships. Anything you find as WAV/MP3 can be converted.
  Mono. 48kHz. Dry — the engine adds distance and space.
  Loops must be SEAMLESS (see the loop lengths in part A for the house sizes).
  CC0 only. No Mojang audio, ever — see the standing rule.

WHERE THEY GO
  public/audio/<name>.ogg, then add the name to SAMPLES in src/audio/Samples.js.
  Loading is fire-and-forget: a missing file is silent and the game falls back
  to the synth, so nothing breaks by adding these one at a time.

  10. Harvest — pulling a ripe crop.
  11. Bone-meal / growth burst, if that exists later.
  12. Whirlpool — the pull, as a continuous bed while you are in one.
  13. Tornado — has ONE call; wants a proper approaching-roar bed.
  14. Lightning STRIKE (the flash's own crack is separate from distant thunder).
  15. Explosion — the synth handles the body, `blast_crack` layers, but the
      Explosion system itself makes no call. Worth checking it fires at all.
  16. Season change / first snow.
  17. Sleeping in a bed / setting spawn — currently a generic UI blip.
  18. Eating finishing (the swallow) — the chew loop exists, the end does not.

  28. Burning (on fire) — a continuous crackle on the player, not the world.
  29. Falling — wind rush past a long drop, before the landing.
  30. Ladder climbing.
  31. Swimming underwater ambience (muffled bed) — `swim` is the stroke only.
  32. Inventory: pick up / put down a stack (currently one generic `ui` blip for

  33. Crate opening / closing.
  34. Kitchen: the dish coming out (separate from `craft`).

PART C — SYNTHESISED, WOULD BENEFIT FROM A LAYERED SAMPLE
-----------------------------------------------------------
These already sound like something. A recording layered on top would give them
the detail a synth cannot: grain, body, the irregularity of a real object.
Ordered by how often a player hears them.

  step         footsteps, per material — stone, wood, grass, sand, snow, soil,
               gravel, glass, metal. NINE surfaces; one sample per surface is
               the single biggest win in this list.
  dig          the mining loop, per material (same nine).
  break_       a block breaking, per material (same nine).
  place        a block being set down, per material (same nine).
  swing        a tool swung through air.
  impact       landing after a fall.
  splash       entering water.
  swim         a stroke.
  surface      breaking the surface for air.
  sink         quicksand / powder snow swallowing you.
  churn        deep water movement.
  hurt         the player taking a hit.
  death        the player dying.
  pickup       an item entering the bag.
  eat          the chew.
  craft        something made at a bench.
  smelt        the kiln finishing.
  kiln         the kiln lighting / going out.
  torchLight   a torch catching.
  door         a door or gate swinging.
  ice          walking on / breaking ice.
  lavaDip      touching lava.
  fuse         a cinderling arming.
  bowDraw      drawing the bow.
  bowRelease   loosing an arrow.
  dryFire      loosing with no arrow.
  levelUp      a skill point earned.
  sunTurn      dawn / dusk marker.
  squall       a weather front arriving.
  saveFail     a save that did not write.
  mob          per-species idle voices (see MOB_SAMPLE in Audio.js for which
               species currently borrow which of the four monster samples —
               the animals have none of their own).
  mobHit       a creature taking a hit.


PART D — ONE SOUND DOING TOO MANY JOBS
----------------------------------------
`audio.ui()` is called from 20 places and `audio.deny()` from 13. Every menu
press, tab change, slot move, screen open, bed claim and toggle is the same
blip. Worth splitting into at least:

  ui.press        a button
  ui.open         a screen opening
  ui.close        a screen closing
  ui.slot         a stack picked up / put down
  ui.tab          a tab or page change
  ui.toggle       a setting switching
  deny.generic    refused
  deny.full       bag full specifically

  kenney.nl      — CC0, and we already ship his art; his audio packs are the
                   easiest match for the game's tone.
  opengameart.org — filter licence to CC0.
  sonniss GDC bundles — royalty-free, check each pack's terms.

For part C, what is wanted is usually ONE clean hit per material, not a
variation set: the engine randomises pitch and level per play, so five stone
footsteps will be generated from one good stone footstep.
